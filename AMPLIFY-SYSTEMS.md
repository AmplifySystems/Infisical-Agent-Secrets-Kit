# Amplify Systems

This kit is maintained by [Amplify Systems](https://amplifysystems.io).

We build AI operating systems for teams that want their tools, data, and day-to-day execution to move as one clean system.

The point is not to collect more software. The point is a tighter business: clearer promises, calmer operations, sharper customer journeys, and fewer fragile manual handoffs.

## What Is a Value Engine?

A **Value Engine** is the system that turns attention, trust, and delivery into real customer value.

In simple terms:

- Your **Growth Engine** gets the right customers.
- Your **Fulfillment Engine** makes those customers happy after they buy.
- Your **Value Engine** is both of those working together around a clear promise.

If your AI agents can use tools safely, they can help map, build, operate, and improve those engines. That is why this security kit matters: secrets are not an isolated developer chore. They are part of the trust layer underneath useful automation.

## Where This Kit Fits

This kit helps you give AI agents access to useful tools without handing them raw credentials:

- GitHub for durable project memory.
- CRMs, docs, databases, deployment platforms, workflow tools, chat tools, and other systems through MCP.
- Infisical as the safe secret boundary.
- Claude Code, Codex, Cursor, and other agents as execution partners.

Once the secret layer is safe, the next question is:

> What should these agents actually help your business do?

That is where Value Engines come in.
